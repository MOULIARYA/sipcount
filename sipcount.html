How we calculate

Per prompt: energy × PUE × (cooling water + grid water). Energy comes from the model type and prompt length; PUE is data-centre overhead; water is in litres per kWh.

  • Standard model ≈ 0.75 Wh per 1,000 tokens (Epoch AI 2025; Google & OpenAI 2025 disclosures)
  • Reasoning models ≈ 10× (our estimate, low confidence)
  • Image ≈ 2.9 Wh each (Luccioni et al. 2023)
  • US: PUE 1.15, cooling 0.55 L/kWh, grid 3.142 L/kWh (Li et al. 2023)

The range shown spans vendor-reported lows (≈0.2×) to 2023 academic highs (≈3.9×). Constants version 2026-09-14.
