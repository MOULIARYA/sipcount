The Cloud is on the Ground

AI feels like magic, but it lives on massive, hyper-heated physical servers. To keep from melting, these data centers evaporate millions of liters of fresh water every single day.

Screen Time for Generative AI

You check your phone’s screen time and battery life. Sipcount gives you that same visibility for your prompts. We translate abstract data center math into tangible, everyday metrics—down to the milliliter.

The Goal: Digital Sobriety

By seeing the estimated physical cost of your queries, you can:

  • Write smarter prompts to get answers faster.
  • Use lighter AI models for simple, everyday tasks.
  • Hold infrastructure accountable by knowing your impact.

How the number is worked out

Scope 1 (cooling) = energy × PUE × WUEsite
Scope 2 (grid) = energy × PUE × EWIFgrid
Total = Scope 1 + Scope 2

  • energy (Wh) from model class × tokens. Input and output tokens are counted separately: an output token is weighted 5× an input token (generating an answer is slower, hotter work than reading a question — central estimate of a 3–10× research range), re-normalised so a typical prompt (≈100 in, ≈300 out) stays ≈0.30 Wh on a standard model (Epoch AI 2025; Google 2025). Reasoning ≈ 10× (estimate); image ≈ 2.9 Wh (Luccioni et al. 2023).
  • PUE data-centre overhead; WUEsite on-site cooling water (ISO/IEC 30134-9); EWIFgrid water evaporated generating the region's electricity (WRI 2020; Li et al. 2023).

Range shown spans vendor-reported lows (≈0.2×) to 2023 academic highs (≈3.9×). Constants . Sources in ARCHITECTURE.md.
