About Sipcount

Behind the Cloud

The "Cloud" is on the Ground

Generative AI feels like magic. You type a prompt, and a server in the cloud instantly writes code, drafts essays, or renders artwork. But the cloud isn't in the sky—it's massive, hyper-heated physical infrastructure sitting right here on earth.

The Hidden Cost of Computing

Every AI query requires intense computational power. To keep these hyperscale server racks from melting, data centers evaporate millions of liters of freshwater daily through cooling towers. Meanwhile, the regional power grids feeding them consume even more water to generate that electricity.

We are trading our most vital physical resource for digital convenience. As global economies push for aggressive 7-8% GDP growth, balancing this massive industrial resource drain against municipal water stress is a math problem that simply doesn't add up.

Why Sipcount Exists

You can't manage what you don't measure. Sipcount strips away the abstraction of server telemetry and translates your estimated AI water footprint into tangible, everyday metrics—down to the milliliter.

We are building a movement of digital sobriety. By seeing the actual cost of your prompts, you can write smarter, more efficient queries, choose lighter AI models for simple tasks, and hold tech infrastructure accountable.

How we calculate

Scope 1 (cooling) = energy × PUE × WUEsite
Scope 2 (grid) = energy × PUE × EWIFgrid
Total = Scope 1 + Scope 2

  • energy (Wh) from model class × prompt length: standard ≈ 0.75 Wh per 1,000 tokens (Epoch AI 2025; Google & OpenAI 2025); reasoning ≈ 10× (estimate); image ≈ 2.9 Wh (Luccioni et al. 2023).
  • PUE data-centre overhead; WUEsite on-site cooling water (ISO/IEC 30134-9); EWIFgrid water evaporated generating the region's electricity (WRI 2020; Li et al. 2023).

Range shown spans vendor-reported lows (≈0.2×) to 2023 academic highs (≈3.9×). Constants . Sources in ARCHITECTURE.md.
