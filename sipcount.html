How we calculate

Scope 1 (cooling) = energy × PUE × WUEsite
Scope 2 (grid) = energy × PUE × EWIFgrid
Total = Scope 1 + Scope 2

  • energy (Wh) from model class × prompt length: standard ≈ 0.75 Wh per 1,000 tokens (Epoch AI 2025; Google & OpenAI 2025); reasoning ≈ 10× (estimate); image ≈ 2.9 Wh (Luccioni et al. 2023).
  • PUE data-centre overhead; WUEsite on-site cooling water (ISO/IEC 30134-9); EWIFgrid water evaporated generating the region's electricity (WRI 2020; Li et al. 2023).

Range shown spans vendor-reported lows (≈0.2×) to 2023 academic highs (≈3.9×). Constants . Sources in ARCHITECTURE.md.
