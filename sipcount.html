How we calculate

Per prompt, in millilitres:

Scope 1 (cooling) = energy × PUE × WUEsite
Scope 2 (grid) = energy × PUE × EWIFgrid
Total = Scope 1 + Scope 2

  • energy (Wh) from model class × prompt length: standard ≈ 0.75 Wh per 1,000 tokens (Epoch AI 2025; Google & OpenAI 2025 disclosures); reasoning ≈ 10× (our estimate, low confidence); image ≈ 2.9 Wh (Luccioni et al. 2023).
  • PUE: data-centre overhead (published fleet figures per region).
  • WUEsite (L/kWh, ISO/IEC 30134-9): on-site cooling water per kWh of IT energy — depends on cooling technology.
  • EWIFgrid (L/kWh): water evaporated to generate the region's electricity (WRI 2020, Reig et al.; Li et al. 2023). With hydropower on, reservoir evaporation is counted — this is why hydro-heavy grids can look thirstier than coal grids.
  • Water stress: WRI Aqueduct 4.0 class of the region. Shown as context; it does not change the millilitres.

The range shown spans vendor-reported lows (≈0.2×) to 2023 academic highs (≈3.9×). Constants version . Full sources: ARCHITECTURE.md in the repo.
