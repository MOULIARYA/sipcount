## Artwork round (2026-09-22, evening) — hand-drawn characters, five stages each. Live in v15

Third art direction, and the one that works. Round 1 was original layered SVG; round 2 was 3D claymorphism renders; this is **hand-drawn line art on transparency**, which is what she wanted all along ("like someone has drawn it by hand and then made it into a graphic… no background, just the mascot").

- **Prompts** in `docs/MASCOT-PROMPTS-V2.md` (characters) and `docs/MASCOT-STATES-PROMPTS.md` (the stages). The first photographic direction failed exactly as she said — asking for "a photograph of a hand-made toy" produced a 3D succulent on a wooden table. Replaced with "illustrated by hand, then cleaned up into a flat graphic asset", flat cel shading, hand-inked line of varying weight, and no background at all.
- **Transparency**: Gemini cannot output an alpha channel — every model in that family paints a solid background. So each character was generated on a **flat chromakey field** (magenta for the plant, since green is the character; green for the rest) and keyed here with an HSV mask plus de-fringing. No colour halo survives.
- **Four characters × five stages = twenty frames**, 760 px on the long edge, WebP with alpha, **832 KB for the lot**. Stages map to the day as roughly fine · first signs · halfway · struggling · at the limit.
- **All five stages of a character share one crop**, computed as the union of their bounding boxes in normalised coordinates. Without this the character appears to jump size and position between stages, which is the one thing that makes a cross-fade look broken. It also absorbed a real inconsistency in the source: four of the twenty (`Plant01`, `Bear01`, `Glacier01`, `Leapord03`) came out of the generator at 1K while the rest were 2K.
- **Card rendering changed from `cover` to `contain`** for image mode. The art is now cut-out with transparency and the four characters have very different proportions (bear 0.75:1 portrait, glacier 1.77:1 landscape); cropping to fill would have beheaded the bear. The character now sits whole inside the card with the app's black around it, which is what the cut-out is for.
- Verified: every stage renders, the percentage-to-stage mapping is right, the Settings gallery shows the real artwork, and all four characters remain legible at 62 px in all five stages. **One weakness**: the leopard's stages 1–3 are hard to tell apart — the snow retreats too subtly. Worth regenerating 2 and 3 with more of the rock exposed if she agrees.


**Later the same evening — two adjustments (v15.1).**

**Round 26 (2026-09-22, after the first push).**
